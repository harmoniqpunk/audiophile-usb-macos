Installed

Connect and power on the Audiophile USB, then pick "Audiophile USB (experimental)" in System Preferences > Sound or Audio MIDI Setup.

A diagnostics report named AudiophileUSB-diagnostics-…txt was saved to your Desktop (or /Users/Shared/AudiophileUSB). Please attach it when reporting problems.

To remove everything, open /Library/Application Support/AudiophileUSB/uninstall.command.
